Before you book

  • Check passenger names match the accepted travel document exactly.
  • Ask for the fare, taxes, baggage and service charges in writing.
  • Check transit and entry rules for your nationality and route with the relevant authority.
  • Read change, cancellation and refund conditions before accepting a quotation. An enquiry is not a booking.
