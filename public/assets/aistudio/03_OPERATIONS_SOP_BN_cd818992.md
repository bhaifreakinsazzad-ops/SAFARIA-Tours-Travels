# SAFARIA — B2B Bangladesh Launch Operations SOP

**Operating mode:** WhatsApp-first, agency-owned clients, structured SAFARIA backend, manually verified membership activation. Legal/regulatory study is out of scope of this operations document. Commercial claims remain accurate and must not pretend transactions already occurred.

## Sequence: first lead → first renewal

**Step 1 — Lead:** Visitor completes `Agency Needs Finder`/short form and opens WhatsApp. Record source, agency, district, plan interest and concern (fare/follow-up/payment/refund/trust/growth). Generate reference only if backend persistence succeeded.

**Step 2 — WhatsApp contact:** A human owner confirms the actual inbound message. Do not consider `wa.me` click a received message. Attach matched reference, next action, owner and due date.

**Step 3 — Qualify:** Ask (1) usual route/category, (2) monthly enquiry volume, (3) biggest workflow issue, (4) current team size. Give a guided workspace demo and explain precisely what SAFARIA coordinates.

**Step 4 — Review and trial:** Collect verification details through authorized private onboarding. Staff reviews, then grants 72h trial with recorded start/end and restricted entitlements. No trial on button click.

**Step 5 — Membership offer:** Explain ৳5,999 monthly, or ৳69,999 yearly; the payment/activation pathway is manual until a verified company checkout is enabled. Explain 0% conditional airfare markup without implying zero supplier or ticket cost. Present written scope and support hours.

**Step 6 — Invoice and confirmation:** Create invoice in SAFARIA owner-controlled register, provide approved company collection details. Staff separately verifies actual settlement; mark `VERIFIED_PAID`; another authorized step activates paid access. Screenshot alone never equals verified payment.

**Step 7 — First success:** During onboarding, guide the agency to create its first real FareDesk request with reference, explain follow-up timeline and how BookingDesk / AfterCare retain case records. Do not fake supplier quotes.

**Step 8 — Service operations:** Every relevant request has unique ID, owner, created date, status and next action. Support team uses six desks as the durable record. WhatsApp is a communication surface, not sole ledger.

**Step 9 — Entitlement monitoring:** Show agency 20 fare/8 assisted booking/2 after-sales/2 seats pilot limits; monitor centrally. Notify when near limits, offer review. Do not silently charge, hard stop without explanation or change prior records.

**Step 10 — Renewal:** Send opt-in notices on configured timetable; staff confirms renewal payment, extends period and resets usage only at defined billing boundary. Log grace/suspension explicitly.

## Daily operating loop

- Morning: review new leads, unassigned cases, overdue follow-up and trials ending.
- During business hours: reply with inquiry-specific context and reference; prioritize time-sensitive urgent issues realistically.
- Evening: reconcile new invoices/payment records and pending supplier responses; schedule next day tasks.
- Weekly: review B2B landing traffic, qualified lead rate, first reply latency, demos, trial usage, new paid subscriptions, churn risks and open refund cases.

## Go-live definition

- `/b2b` visible and accurate; WhatsApp link opens; a named human receives actual messages and logs next actions.
- Partners know inquiry is not ticket issuance and application is not activation.
- Do not announce live payment, airline supplier guarantee, automatic delivery or full SaaS entitlement engine unless verified on the deployed system.
