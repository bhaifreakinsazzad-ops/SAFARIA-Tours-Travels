Is my enquiry a confirmed booking?
No. A SAFARIA agent checks availability and fares, then sends you a verified quotation. Your booking is confirmed only after you approve the quote and pay.
Do you guarantee visa approval?
No. We help with documents and coordination. Decisions are made by the relevant government or immigration authority.
How do I reach a person?
WhatsApp, call or SMS the SAFARIA number, or submit the quote form and we'll contact you.
