# Integrity and brand architecture

## Who is who (footer credits, in this order)

| Entity | Role | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SAFARIA Tours & Travels | the travel agency the customer deals with | "Travel services by SAFARIA Tours & Travels" |
| Just Another Travel Group | parent travel ecosystem | "A Just Another Travel Group company" |
| ViBE Trippin' | sister travel experiences brand | "Sister brand: ViBE Trippin'", in its own editorial block with its own CTA |
| NotRealEngine, LLC | builds the digital platform | "Platform by NotRealEngine, LLC", linking to notrealengine.online |

Never merge these into one legal identity, and never route ViBE Trippin' enquiries into SAFARIA forms.

## Never publish

Do not publish invented reviews, customer counts, booking statistics, airline partnerships, IATA membership or government accreditation. Do not publish "lowest price" guarantees, guaranteed ticket issuance, guaranteed visa approval, 24/7 availability, refund guarantees, live fares, or live flight data that doesn't come from a licensed provider. Where testimonials are missing, omit the section. Don't add placeholder quotes.

## Required disclaimers

- **Destinations:** "Destinations are enquiry options. Routes and airlines are confirmed in your quotation."
- **B2B:** "0% SAFARIA markup on eligible air tickets. Airfare, taxes and supplier charges remain payable. Prepaid settlement. Not a lowest-fare guarantee; ticketing subject to supplier availability and fare rules." Show ৳20,000/month, ৳130,000/year and the 72-hour trial as "current terms, subject to confirmation".
- **Visa:** "Decisions are made by the relevant government or immigration authority."
- **AI assistant:** "AI guidance only. Prices, availability and visa rules are confirmed by a SAFARIA agent."
- **Tracker / globe:** "Illustrative" unless the data really comes from a licensed live aviation API, in which case show its last-updated time.
